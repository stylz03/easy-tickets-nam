# Namibia photography

These are documentary photographs of real Namibian locations, used as atmospheric editorial imagery. They do not depict the sample events or their venues.

- `sossusvlei-dunes.jpg` — Sossusvlei dunes, photograph by Vicartb. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sossusvlei_Dunes_Namib.jpg), CC0 1.0.
